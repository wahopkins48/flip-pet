# Elderflip launch notes

Positioning: **A small cosmic horror for your dumbphone.**

Lead with a photo or short clip of the real flip phone. Seeing the physical
keypad, tiny creature, and four actions explains the appeal faster than a long
feature list. Show a naturally hatched pet, or label any accelerated test save
as a demo. Keep Gentle mode visible.

## Two audiences

- **r/dumbphones:** phone compatibility, physical keys, offline operation, and a pet that tolerates time away. Draft: `reddit-dumbphones.md`.
- **r/SideProject:** the absurdly specific premise, creature personality, and candid AI-assisted development. Draft: `reddit-sideproject.md`.

Do not submit these AI-assisted drafts to **r/Lovecraft**. Its published rules
prohibit AI-generated and AI-adjacent content, including text. It also has
separate self-promotion restrictions. Rewriting the disclosure does not resolve
that community's restriction.

3. If offering a direct download, publish a clearly versioned release and then add its real URL to the posts. No release download URL is assumed here.
4. Add a real phone photo/clip. Invite compatibility reports with phone model and Android version.
